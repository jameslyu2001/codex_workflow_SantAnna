# Referee report style

1. Summarize the paper's question, method, and main claim in your own words.
2. State the recommendation near the beginning and identify its single load-bearing reason.
3. Number major concerns. Treat each as a threat to a conclusion, cite the exact section, equation, table, figure, code output, or page, and add “What would change my mind” with a concrete ask.
4. Separate fatal problems, addressable problems, and matters of taste. Do not present a defensible alternative choice as an error merely because you would choose differently.
5. Put clarity, notation, citations, and presentation issues under minor concerns.
6. Acknowledge work that is genuinely strong. Critique claims and methods, never authors.

Do not invent flaws or evidence. If a concern cannot be tied to a specific location or source-backed implication, mark it as a question or omit it.
